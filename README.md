# 60-days-Claude-Challenge
Hi, I'm Wahib Ejaz — a final-year BSIT student and frontend developer.
How I'm Taking This Challenge
I think in systems and love going deep on ideas — but my honest weakness has always been finishing what I start. Plenty of brilliant threads, fewer shipped outcomes.
That's exactly why I'm here. This challenge isn't about learning AI in theory — it's about building the daily-shipping muscle in public, where I can't quietly drop it.
My approach:

🧩 Build small, ship daily — a working thing every day beats a perfect thing never finished
🧠 Learn by interrogation — I don't just prompt for answers, I stress-test ideas with "then what happens?"
📓 Document honestly — real learnings and dead-ends, not just polished outputs
🔁 Protect the streak — consistency is the whole point; momentum is the reward

By Day 60 I want a portfolio of shipped builds, a prompting playbook that actually works, and proof — to myself and to employers — that I finish what I start.
